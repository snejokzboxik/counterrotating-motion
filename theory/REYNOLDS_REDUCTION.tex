\section{Reynolds group and controlled linear-drag limit}
For $\boldsymbol\xi_i=\mathbf r_i/R$ and $\tau=\Omega_1t$, the diameter Reynolds number is $Re_i=Re_{*,i}|\boldsymbol\xi_i'|$ with $Re_{*,i}=2\rho_fa_iR\Omega_1/\eta$. The exact coordinate change $\boldsymbol\zeta_i=Re_{*,i}\boldsymbol\xi_i$ makes the nonlinear drag argument $|\boldsymbol\zeta_i'|$, but a spatial RF term of degree $n$ acquires factor $Re_{*,i}^{2-n}$; Coulomb, contact and aperture retain particle-specific scales. Thus the transformation does not reduce the physical dimension of the full parameter set.

The replacement $1+0.15Re^{0.687}\simeq1$ is controlled only when the instantaneous, RF-resolved Reynolds correction is small. At the nominal M3 collocation state the measured median $Re$ is 0.332 and 1.605 for the two particles, and the paired M3 linear-drag control changes the frequency magnitudes by 25.8\% and 11.1\%. The signs happen to persist in this paired solve; that is a result for the tested branch, not a general invariance theorem.
