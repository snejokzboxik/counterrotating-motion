\section{Radius existence and elimination}
For normalized leading harmonics $k=+2,-4$, define
\begin{align}
U_i&=\alpha_i\beta-4\alpha_i^2A^2K_{1i}/D_{1i},\\
V_i&=48\alpha_i^2B^2K_{2i}/D_{2i}.
\end{align}
The leading radial force is $F_{r,i}=r_i(U_i-V_i r_i^4)$. A positive finite root exists iff $U_i>0,V_i>0$; then $r_i^4=U_i/V_i$. For non-unit MFS coefficients insert $|c_2|^2$ in the RF1 term of $U_i$ and $|c_{-4}|^2$ in $V_i$. The strict geometric condition is $r_i+a_i/R<r_{\rm ap}/R$.

At fixed zero spin, $\partial_{r_i}F_{r,i}|_*=U_i-5V_ir_i^4=-4U_i<0$. This is a local restoring slope of a scalar force section, not the stability eigenvalue of the full slow system. Algebraically solving for $r_i$ does not remove radial dynamics.
