\section{Finite linear drag and nonlinear response}
Retaining finite $\lambda_i$ and $\alpha_i\beta$ in the leading susceptibility gives
\begin{equation}
S_i=\frac{\nu}{K_{2i}}\left[\frac{\beta D_{1i}}{4\alpha_iA^2|c_2|^2}-K_{1i}\right].
\end{equation}
Within this model $B$ and $|c_{-4}|$ cancel from $S_i$, but the radial root depends on both and may be lost or cross the aperture. Nonzero orbit speed, SN drag, higher MFS modes, radial mean drag and Coulomb modify both radial and angular balances; they cannot be absorbed into this formula without re-solving the averaged system. In particular, a torque-sign argument only transfers to an angular-velocity sign under monotone local torque with positive effective damping.
